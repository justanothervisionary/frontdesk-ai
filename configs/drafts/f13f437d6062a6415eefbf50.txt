Swigable is a smart water bottle currently available for pre-order on Kickstarter. The bottle tracks your hydration automatically in real-time and uses AI-powered coaching to help build consistent drinking habits. It features a 450ml stainless steel bottle with a waterproof magnetic health device, up to 2 days of battery life, and works independently without requiring the app, though an app is available to enhance the experience with insights and tracking. The bottle comes in four colors: Ocean, Candy Floss, Carbon, and Forest. Pre-orders are offered at 54% off and are fully refundable, limited to the first 300 spots. Each order includes the smart bottle, charging cable, and quick start guide. The product is currently in pre-production with delivery timelines to be confirmed. Swigable is designed for everyday use at home, work, or on-the-go and is safe for children with parental supervision. Multiple bottles can be ordered per reservation.
