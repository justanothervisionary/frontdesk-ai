Swigable is a smart hydration tracking system featuring AI-powered personalized hydration profiling, real-time sip detection via advanced sensors, and a built-in high-resolution display on compatible bottles and containers. The app generates scientifically tailored daily hydration goals based on age, weight, lifestyle, and local weather conditions. Users receive context-aware reminders, track progress through gamified streaks and achievements, and benefit from an AI companion coach (Swiggy) that evolves with usage patterns. The system includes weather-based automatic goal adjustments and a modular magnetic design allowing the smart screen to attach to bottles, mugs, and wearable accessories. Upcoming features include social leaderboards and hydration challenges.
