Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This describes how Frontdesk ("we," "us") handles data when you use our website, our self-serve tools, and the AI receptionist widget installed on a client's website. We've written this in plain language on purpose - the full technical detail lives in our security overview, written for whoever handles IT on your side.

Who this covers

Two different groups of people interact with Frontdesk, and this policy covers both:

  • Businesses who sign up for Frontdesk to add an AI receptionist to their own website.
  • Visitors who chat with a Frontdesk widget installed on one of our clients' websites.

What we collect from businesses signing up

  • Your email address, business name, and the details you choose to give your AI receptionist (services, hours, phone number, an optional photo or business description).
  • If you use the "read my website" option instead of typing this in yourself, we fetch the text of the page you point us to and send it to Anthropic to summarize into your AI receptionist's training information - the same provider used to power the chat itself. We don't store a copy of your website; only the summary you review and can edit before it goes live.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Stripe - we never see or store your card details. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what they hold.
  • If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Google asks you to approve access limited to checking free/busy times and creating events - never reading your existing event details. The access this grants is stored securely on our servers, never in anything a visitor's browser can reach, and you can disconnect it at any time from your dashboard or from your own Google Account settings.

What we collect from a visitor chatting with a client's widget

  • Whatever the visitor types into the chat, sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply. Only the current conversation is sent - never anything else on the page, never browsing history.
  • If a visitor chooses to leave their name and contact details (always their own choice, never collected automatically), that's emailed directly to the business along with the last few messages of the conversation, so the business has context on what was asked. The name, contact, and that short excerpt of the conversation are also kept in a short-lived log, deleted automatically after 35 days, so the business can review it and get a weekly summary of new leads in their dashboard.
  • If the AI couldn't answer a question from the business's own information, the question itself (not who asked it) is logged separately so the business can see what's missing from their FAQs - kept only as a short rolling list, not tied to any visitor's identity.
  • If a visitor chooses to attach a photo or PDF (for example, a picture of the issue they're asking about), that file is stored and a link to it is included in the lead notification sent to the business, the same as the name/contact details above - always the visitor's own choice, never required to use the chat.
  • No cookies, no tracking scripts, no analytics on the widget itself.

What we collect from a visitor messaging a client's WhatsApp number

  • Some businesses also offer an AI receptionist over WhatsApp. Messages are relayed through Meta's own WhatsApp Business Platform and sent to Anthropic the same way as the website widget, to generate a reply.
  • Your WhatsApp number is already your own contact detail on that platform - we only ever use it to reply to your conversation and, if it's a genuine enquiry, to let the business know, the same as the lead-capture description above.
  • A short rolling window of recent messages is kept per conversation so the AI has context across your messages (WhatsApp doesn't give us anything like that itself) - deleted automatically after 48 hours of inactivity.

What we collect from a visitor booking an appointment

  • Where a business has connected Google Calendar, the AI can offer real appointment times and book one directly. Confirming a time sends your name and contact details (whatever you've given in the conversation) to that business's calendar as the event's details, and to the business by email - the same as the lead-capture description above, just with a specific time attached.
  • We don't read, store, or share anything else on the business's calendar - access is limited to checking which times are free and creating the one event your booking needs.

How long we keep things

Business configs (what your AI receptionist knows) are kept for as long as your subscription is active, in a version-controlled repository. A lead's short conversation excerpt is deleted automatically after 35 days. Unanswered-question entries are kept only as a short rolling list (oldest dropped off automatically as new ones come in), and are never tied to who asked. Cancelling your subscription takes your AI receptionist offline immediately.

Who we share data with

  • Stripe - payment processing.
  • Anthropic - powers the AI that answers chat messages.
  • Resend - delivers lead-notification and login emails.
  • Meta - relays messages for businesses using the WhatsApp channel.
  • Google - checks calendar availability and creates events for businesses that connect Google Calendar.
  • Upstash - stores usage counts, the unanswered-question list mentioned above, and the short-lived WhatsApp conversation window.
  • Vercel and GitHub - host the site and store configs. Vercel Blob storage also holds any file a visitor chooses to attach to a conversation.

We don't sell data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond what's needed to actually run the service.
