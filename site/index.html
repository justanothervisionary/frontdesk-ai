Who's behind this

Built by people who had the same problem

We're Danny and Jade, based in West London. Between us we've spent years building marketing systems and automation - first inside our own business, solving our own problem: too many enquiries falling through the cracks outside office hours, and not enough time to answer everything ourselves.

Frontdesk started as a tool we built for that. Once it worked for us, it was obvious most local businesses are sitting on the exact same problem - and that AI and automation, done properly, isn't the overhyped, confusing thing it's often made out to be. We built Frontdesk to make it simple: one AI receptionist that actually helps, not a chatbot with your logo stuck on it.
